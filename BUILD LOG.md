Started by trying to revive the existing Meccano robot. It was covered in dust and looked like any robot that hadn't been touched in 10 years would look.

I figured the battery was dead and looked up the manual to figure out how to charge it. I quickly learned that it has a custom battery pack and charger which I didn't have anymore. Bonus, the product and its charger are non longer manufactured. I started looking for replacement chargers sold locally online. I was also considering buying a whole new battery pack as I wasn't sure the original one still works.

Since that was a bust, I started looking at how I could move the servos on it with an Arduino. I noticed some people made open source libraries for controlling these types of motors from an Arduino. The one I decided to start with is documented here. https://github.com/alexfrederiksen/MeccanoidForArduino/tree/master

But before I could get to using it, I had to figure out how to even connect a Meccanoid servo to an Arduino Uno. For that, I stumbled upon this helpful video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8y_xMW0Kr5c. The video was creaed by MECCAControl which apparently makes a Windows software you can use to easily program Meccanoid components, but I have a mac.
