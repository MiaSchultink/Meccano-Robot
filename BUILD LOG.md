Started by trying to revive the existing Meccano robot. It was covered in dust and looked like any robot that hadn't been touched in 10 years would look.

I figured the battery was dead and looked up the manual to figure out how to charge it. I quickly learned that it has a custom battery pack and charger which I didn't have anymore. Bonus, the product and its charger are non longer manufactured.

Since that was a bust, I started looking at how I could move the servos on it with an Arduino. A little research revealed that it has custom servos and an Arduno library.
